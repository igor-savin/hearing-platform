A few types of medicine can damage those cells or irritate the nerve pathways nearby. When that happens, the first sign is often a high-pitched ringing or a sense that voices have lost some of their clarity. Sometimes the effect is temporary and eases after you finish the medication. In other cases, especially with certain strong drugs at high doses, the change can be permanent. The dose, the length of treatment, and whether you take more than one ototoxic drug at once all shape how much risk there is."],
