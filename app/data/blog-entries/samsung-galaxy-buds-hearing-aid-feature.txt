It is a self-administered test that takes about five minutes and uses pure-tone audiometry, tested one ear at a time, to produce an audiogram. The second is the Hearing Aid feature, which amplifies sound according to your results. It was cleared through the FDA's over-the-counter pathway, the same category as OTC hearing aids sold in stores."],
      ["How it works", "You take the test in the app with the earbuds in. The app sorts the result into normal or one of four stages of loss, from mild to profound, and builds an audiogram. The amplification then uses NAL-NL2, a fitting formula from Australia's National Acoustic Laboratories that clinics widely use to set prescription hearing aids. The system also includes beamforming microphones that favor sound in front of you and noise reduction aimed at speech. Wareable reports that the audiogram is stored in the Samsung Health app."],

A 30-day study of 103 adults with mild to moderate loss found that people who fit the feature themselves reported benefit that was not worse than people fitted by a clinician.
