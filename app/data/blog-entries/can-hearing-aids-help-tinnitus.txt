Because the relief comes from the sound around you rather than from noise piped into your ear, it feels natural, and it often carries over for a while even after you take the aids out."],

This is the same idea behind [sound therapy](/tinnitus/sound-therapy/), delivered through a device you already wear all day. An audiologist can set the pitch and volume of these sounds to sit comfortably alongside your own tinnitus and refine them over follow-up visits. Some people find the amplification alone is enough; others lean on the sound programs during quiet evenings or at their desk."],
