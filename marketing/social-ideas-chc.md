# CHC social ideas list (Facebook, Instagram, Google posts)

A living list. **Add a line whenever a new idea comes up.** Claude reads this file
before it plans any CHC social post, and adds ideas it notices while working.
Site pages come first. Every post links to a live page on calhearing.com.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

## Rules for every post

- Use "linked to" and "may". Never write "causes", "protects" or "prevents".
- Do not copy other people's images or text. Redraw from public numbers, cite, and link out.
- Use the CHC teal palette (teal #155f6b, aqua #86d0d5, cream #fbf7ef). Do not use the retired orange palette.
- One Facebook Page covers both offices. Google posts need one version per office, each with its own phone number.
- Graphics live as SVG in `public/assets/core/hearing-better__*.svg`. Make a square PNG when a post is scheduled.
- Put the source on the graphic when it shows numbers. Say "population estimate, not personal risk" for Lancet numbers.
- Check the review date on the linked page. Do not post from a page older than one year.

## Status key

`idea` = not started. `ready` = graphic and page exist, caption not written. `scheduled` = in Meta Business Suite. `posted` = done, add the date.

## Things to watch for (add new items here as they come up)

- The Lancet Commission updates its report. Check once a year and redraw the chart if the numbers change.
- A product on a page changes price or availability. Update the page before posting.
- A maker sends money or a discount for a promotion. Say so in the post.
- Patient stories need written consent.
