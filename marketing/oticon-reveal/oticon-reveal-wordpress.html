Oticon Reveal™

The World's First Hearing Aid Powered by Dual AI

Two dedicated AI systems working together in real time. One brings you crystal-clear speech. The other keeps the world around you intact.

Two AI Systems. One Complete Sound Picture.

Announced by Oticon on August 11, 2026, the Oticon Reveal represents a fundamental rethink of what artificial intelligence should do inside a hearing aid. For years, the industry has taken a single approach to difficult listening environments: identify speech, then aggressively suppress everything else. It makes for an impressive demonstration in a quiet clinic. In real life, it strips away the sounds that tell your brain where you are.

Oticon Reveal takes a different path. It runs two dedicated AI systems simultaneously. A Speech AI continuously identifies and enhances the speech of people around you. At the same time, a Context AI recognizes and balances the meaningful sounds that give a room its shape — footsteps, a door, traffic, the ambient life of a restaurant. Neither one waits for the other. Both run all day, in every environment, in real time.

The result, according to Oticon's research, is a 60% stronger brain response than Oticon Intent — the company's previous flagship and one of the most respected hearing aids on the market. Highlights of Oticon Reveal — courtesy of Oticon Professionals

Advanced Technology: The Reveal AI™ Platform

Oticon Reveal is built on an entirely new foundation. The Reveal AI platform is the company's third-generation AI-powered sound processing system, and it is what makes running two independent AI engines at once possible without draining the battery or growing the device. Alongside it, Oticon introduced a separate, all-new connectivity platform — a genuine redesign rather than an incremental update.

Speech AI: Access to the Voices That Matter

The Speech AI works continuously to identify and enhance the speech of the people around you. Oticon describes it as offering industry-leading access to speech compared to the top three competing manufacturers.

Crucially, it does this without shutting the rest of the world down. Speech is brought forward, not carved out of silence — which is what allows your brain to keep track of who is speaking, from where, and what else is happening while they do.

Context AI: The World Stays Where It Belongs

Running in parallel, the Context AI recognizes and balances the surrounding contextual sounds that most hearing aids treat as noise to be eliminated. Your brain relies on these cues constantly and unconsciously — to orient you in a space, to track movement behind you, and to reduce the effort of listening.

This is BrainHearing™ thinking taken a step further: support the brain with a complete, balanced sound scene rather than an artificially narrowed one.

A 60% Stronger Brain Response

Oticon's clinical research found that Reveal delivers a 60% stronger brain response than Oticon Intent, attributing the result to what the company calls the world's best combination of the fastest and most precise AI sound processing available.

In practical terms, a stronger brain response means your auditory system is receiving a clearer, more complete signal to work with — which is what reduces listening fatigue over a long day of conversation.

Smarter Feedback Prevention

Reveal's feedback management system is 30% faster than previous Oticon technology and delivers four times the fitting precision. For your audiologist, that extra precision means more usable gain can be prescribed before feedback becomes a limitation — which directly benefits patients with more significant hearing loss or open fittings.

Best-Ever Streaming Stability

The new connectivity platform provides enhanced signal strength and Oticon's best-ever streaming stability and range for hands-free calls and audio streaming — addressing one of the most common day-to-day frustrations hearing aid wearers report.

A New CROS Solution for Single-Sided Deafness

Alongside Reveal, Oticon is introducing a new CROS device. CROS systems are designed for people with little or no usable hearing in one ear: a discreet transmitter worn on the non-hearing side picks up sound and routes it wirelessly to a hearing aid on the better ear, restoring awareness of an entire side of your world.

Pairing a new CROS with Reveal's Dual AI is a meaningful step for these patients, because context and spatial awareness are exactly what single-sided deafness takes away. If you have been told a CROS system was your only option — or that it would not help much — this is worth a fresh conversation with our audiologists.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dual AI, and why does it matter?

Dual AI means Oticon Reveal runs two dedicated artificial intelligence systems at the same time instead of one. The Speech AI continuously identifies and enhances the speech of people around you, while the Context AI recognizes and balances the meaningful sounds that make up your surroundings. Most hearing aids use a single AI focused on isolating speech and aggressively suppressing everything else. Reveal is the first to give context its own dedicated processing, so your brain receives a complete sound picture rather than a narrowed one.

How is Oticon Reveal different from Oticon Intent?

Reveal is built on an entirely new platform — the Reveal AI platform, Oticon's third-generation AI sound processing system — while Intent uses the Sirius platform with Deep Neural Network 2.0. Oticon's research measured a 60% stronger brain response with Reveal compared to Intent. Reveal also adds an all-new connectivity platform with improved streaming stability and range, and faster, far more precise feedback prevention.

What does "60% stronger brain response" actually mean?

Oticon measures brain response using research that observes how clearly and completely the brain receives and processes the sound signal a hearing aid delivers. A stronger response indicates the auditory system is getting a richer, more usable signal to work with. Practically, patients tend to experience this as less listening effort and less fatigue at the end of a long day of conversation. This figure compares Reveal to Oticon Intent.

Does Oticon Reveal support Auracast?

Yes. Auracast broadcast technology is built into Oticon Reveal through Bluetooth LE Audio. Auracast allows you to connect directly to public audio broadcasts in venues like theaters, airports, lecture halls, museums, and places of worship — the audio streams straight to your hearing aids instead of competing with room noise. Auracast venues are expanding steadily across the U.S., and the Bay Area is among the earlier adopters.

Can I take phone calls hands-free with Reveal?

Yes. Oticon Reveal supports hands-free calling and direct audio streaming, and its new connectivity platform delivers Oticon's best-ever streaming stability and range. That means you can leave your phone on the table and still take the call clearly.

Is Oticon Reveal rechargeable, and how long does the battery last?

Yes, Reveal uses a rechargeable battery designed to deliver full-day power on a single charge, even with the Dual AI system running continuously. Oticon notes that actual battery performance varies based on usage, particularly how much you stream. We will confirm exact runtime figures for your specific configuration at your fitting appointment.

What style does Oticon Reveal come in?

Oticon Reveal launched in a compact, rechargeable miniRITE style — a discreet receiver-in-the-ear design worn behind the ear. A new CROS device for single-sided deafness is launching alongside it. If additional styles become available, we will update this page and our patients accordingly.

How much does Oticon Reveal cost?

Oticon is releasing Reveal across four technology levels, so pricing varies with the level of processing you need. The right level depends on how demanding your listening environments are — someone who works in open offices and eats out frequently benefits from a higher level than someone in quieter surroundings. We discuss pricing transparently at your consultation and will help you find the level that gives you the most benefit for your budget. Call (650) 342-9449 for current pricing.

Should I choose Oticon Reveal or Oticon Zeal?

It comes down to your first priority. Zeal is the answer when discretion is the deciding factor — it is invisible or barely visible in 94% of users. Reveal is the answer when you want the most advanced sound processing available and you are comfortable with a discreet behind-the-ear design. Both offer Bluetooth LE Audio and Auracast. Our audiologists will help you weigh the trade-off during your consultation.

I have hearing in only one ear. Can Reveal help?

Possibly, yes. Oticon is launching a new CROS device alongside Reveal, designed specifically for single-sided deafness. A CROS system captures sound from your non-hearing side and routes it wirelessly to a device on your better ear, restoring awareness of a side of the world you have been missing. Schedule an evaluation and we will assess whether a CROS configuration is appropriate for you.
