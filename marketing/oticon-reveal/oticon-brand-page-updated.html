Groundbreaking Oticon Technology

BrainHearing™: A Revolutionary Approach

Oticon's philosophy centers around their proprietary BrainHearing™ approach, which acknowledges that hearing happens in the brain, not just the ears. Traditional hearing aids focus primarily on making sounds louder, but Oticon's technology is designed to reduce cognitive load and provide the brain with clearer sound signals.

This brain-first approach means you'll experience:

  • Less listening effort in complex sound environments
  • Better speech recognition, even with background noise
  • More natural sound quality that preserves important details
  • Reduced listening fatigue after long conversations

Oticon Reveal™: The World's First Hearing Aid Powered by Dual AI

For years, the entire hearing industry has taken one approach to noisy rooms: find the speech, then aggressively suppress everything else. It demonstrates beautifully in a quiet clinic. In a real restaurant, it strips away the sounds that tell your brain where you are.

Oticon Reveal, announced in August 2026, does something no hearing aid has done before. It runs two dedicated AI systems simultaneously. A Speech AI continuously identifies and enhances the speech of the people around you. At the same time, a Context AI recognizes and balances the meaningful sounds that give a room its shape. Neither waits for the other, and both run all day in every environment.

Built on the all-new Reveal AI™ platform — Oticon's third-generation AI sound processing system — the Reveal delivers a 60% stronger brain response than Oticon Intent, the company's previous flagship and one of the most respected hearing aids on the market.

Oticon Zeal™: The World's First "Unseen" Hearing Aid to Do It All

The Oticon Zeal represents a design revolution, creating a brand-new category known as NXT In-the-Ear technology. For the first time ever, you don't have to choose between invisibility and performance. The Zeal delivers the complete connectivity, rechargeability, and AI-powered sound processing of a premium behind-the-ear device—all in a size so discreet it's invisible or barely visible in 94% of users.

Groundbreaking Features

Oticon Intent™: Revolutionary User-Intent Sensors

The Intent™ family features the world's first user-intent sensors—a groundbreaking technology that recognizes when your listening needs change and adapts automatically. These intelligent devices can:

  • Detect when you're having a conversation and enhance speech clarity
  • Recognize when you're listening to music and optimize sound quality
  • Automatically adjust settings based on your environment
  • Provide seamless transitions between different listening situations

Intent remains an outstanding choice, and it is available across a wider range of styles and technology levels than the newer models—which makes it the most flexible option for many budgets.
