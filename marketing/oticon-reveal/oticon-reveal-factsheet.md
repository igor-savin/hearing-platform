# Oticon Reveal™ — Research Brief
**Prepared for California Hearing Center · August 11, 2026**

> **Context:** Oticon announced Reveal on **August 11, 2026 — today.** Only two primary sources exist so far (Oticon's consumer press release and Demant's investor announcement), plus an official Oticon Professionals video. Trade press (HearingReview, HearingTracker, AudiologyOnline) has not yet covered it, and oticon.com has no Reveal product page live. Everything below is separated into **Confirmed** and **Not Yet Disclosed** so nothing unverified ends up on the site.

---

## 1. The one-line positioning

**Oticon Reveal is the world's first hearing aid powered by Dual AI** — two dedicated AI systems running simultaneously: one for speech, one for everything else.

This is a genuine category story, not a spec bump. Oticon is explicitly positioning *against* the rest of the industry:

> "Until now, the majority of the hearing aid industry has prioritised speech by utilising aggressive AI noise reduction."

That is the hook for the page. Competitors strip the world away to isolate speech. Reveal keeps the world and clarifies the speech inside it.

## 4. Three traps to avoid

**1. The "25 dB more gain" claim is not in any primary source.**
AuraHear's write-up mentions "up to 25 dB more gain than some competing hearing aids." That figure appears in **no** Oticon or Demant material. Leave it off the page, or you'll be defending a number Oticon never made.

**2. AccessGUDID "Oticon Reveal" records are a false positive.**
GUDID lists ~98 Oticon A/S devices branded "Reveal" with descriptions like `G40, MINIRITE T 312 2.4G C090 REVEAL`. These are **zinc-air 312/13-battery, non-LE-Audio** devices with DI records published **31 May 2017** — a legacy OEM/private-label family, unrelated to the 2026 flagship. Do not use them as a model lineup.

**3. "Reveal 1 / 2 / 3 / 4" is an inference, not a fact.**
Demant said "four price points." The tier *names* are unpublished. Say "four technology levels" rather than naming them until Oticon confirms.

## 5. Reveal vs. Zeal vs. Intent — how to position them together

This matters for your internal linking, because you now have three current Oticon flagships and patients will ask which one.

| | **Oticon Reveal** | **Oticon Zeal** | **Oticon Intent** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The pitch** | Most advanced sound processing | Most discreet | The proven all-rounder |
| **Platform** | Reveal AI (3rd-gen AI) | Sirius | Sirius |
| **AI** | Dual AI — Speech AI + Context AI | Deep Neural Network 2.0 | Deep Neural Network 2.0 |
| **Style** | Compact miniRITE, rechargeable | CIC / "NXT In-the-Ear" | miniRITE R and others |
| **Visibility** | Behind the ear, discreet | Invisible or barely visible in 94% of users | Behind the ear |
| **Auracast** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **CROS option** | Yes — new CROS launching alongside | No | Yes |
| **Best for** | Patients who want the strongest speech-in-noise performance available | Patients for whom discretion is the deciding factor | Patients who want proven tech at a broader range of price points |

**The clean way to explain it to a patient:** Zeal is the answer to "I don't want anyone to see it." Reveal is the answer to "I want the best hearing I can get." They're not competing — they're two different first questions.
