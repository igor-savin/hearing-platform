# Competitor facts

Verified as of **August 2026**. Every launch comparison draws on this, so it
has to stay current — **re-verify anything older than about three months
before printing it**, and update this file when you do.

The traps are as important as the facts. Each one below is a number that
circulates widely and is wrong, or a source that contradicts itself.

## The through-line worth keeping

**A neural chip does not predict measured performance.** Widex Allure has no
DNN in its sound path at all and ranks #7 of 36 tested; ReSound Vivia has a
dedicated one and ranks #20, below the category average. Signia MaX has four
and has never been measured.

This is the single most useful thing either practice can tell a patient in
2026, because every manufacturer is now marketing on neural-network count.

## Current flagships

**Phonak EON** (24 Aug 2026, Stäfa; US same day, Germany/France/UK/Australia
Sept 2026). New **HYPERSONIC** chip replacing the two-chip DEEPSONIC design.
Three RIC models: **Audéo EON Sphere, Audéo EON R, CROS EON** — rechargeable
only, up to **38 h**, eight colours, receivers S/M/P/UP/MAV (UP c-shell only).
Bluetooth Classic + LE Audio + RogerDirect + **Auracast working day one** (QR in
myPhonak). **No telecoil** — Audéo never has. Target 12.0, Adaptive Phonak
Digital 4.0, NAL-NL3 supported. Tiers **90/70/50**, and Spheric Speech Clarity
3.0 now reaches **50**, where the DNN was previously top-two only; WindBlock 2.0
across all levels. No independent data, no price, no stated IP rating, **no
mention of tinnitus**.
*The wedge, and it is the best one found at any launch so far:* the headline
claim — **"twice as likely to understand every word from any direction"** —
is footnoted to **Wright et al. (2024)**, a Field Study News about Spheric
Speech Clarity on the **previous** platform, and the footnote's own comparator
is **SSC on versus SSC off**. It is not a measurement of EON, of Infinio Ultra,
or of any competitor. The competitive claim ("outperforms four other hearing
aids", **Ulloa Sanchez et al. 2026, Phonak Insight**) never names the four.
37% / 25% / 20% are footnoted baselines against Infinio Ultra, not studies.
*Second wedge:* **Phonak is not claiming a speech-in-noise jump over Infinio
Ultra** — unusual restraint, and it reframes the whole upgrade question.

**Phonak Audéo Infinio Ultra Sphere** (22 Oct 2025; HearAdvisor files it as
"Sphere Infinio Ultra"). Separate DEEPSONIC chip, ~7.7bn ops/sec, 4.5M
parameters. **Re-checked 24 Aug 2026 and the old numbers were stale:**
HearAdvisor now scores it **4.9/5, grade A, #2 in its category** of the 36
prescription hearing aids tested. Speech in noise **3.1/5 at the manufacturer's
initial fit → 5.0/5 tuned**, against a **category average of 1.96/5**. That
initial-to-tuned jump is now the strongest real-ear-measurement argument on the
site — better than ReSound Vivia's, because it is a device CHC actually sells.
**No telecoil, Auracast not enabled.** Phonak publishes **up to 56 h**, with the
same footnote EON's 38 h carries (runtime depends on the activated feature set),
so **do not compare the two headline numbers directly** — running the DNN was
the previous platform's largest drain. RIC-rechargeable only. Still catalogued
alongside EON, with Virto R Infinio, the titanium Virto models and CROS Infinio.
*Traps:* do not print the "53× more processing power" claim (retailer blogs
only); do not print **"highest recorded speech-in-noise score"** — that was true
of an earlier HearAdvisor page and is not supportable now. Three live CHC pages
carried it and were corrected on 24 Aug 2026.

**Starkey Omega AI** (9 Oct 2025). Grade **A, #2 of 36** tested. Tiers 24/20/16,
G3 Gen AI Neuro Processor, DNN 360 directionality. Widest styles: custom
ITE/ITC/CIC and disposable 312. LE Audio + Auracast shipping. Fall detection,
balance, respiratory rate. Buttons small and stiff — a real dexterity caution.
*Trap:* do not print its HearAdvisor sub-scores; sources contradicted each
other. Only grade and rank were stable.

**Widex Allure**. **No DNN in the sound path**, and still **#7 of 36** with
speech-in-noise 2.5 tuned. **No tinnitus program** (Zen is Moment-only) —
usually decisive for both practices. Auracast pending. BTE and rechargeable
custom available. Allure **AI** (on-demand Clarity Boost, L-RNN co-processor)
reaches the US **1 Nov 2026** — not purchasable yet.

**ReSound Vivia**. Dedicated DNN chip and still **1.3/5 speech-in-noise tuned,
#20 of 36**, below the 1.55 average. Its initial-fit score was **0.6 → 1.3
after tuning**, which is the best real-ear-measurement argument on either site.
*Trap:* a retailer blog claiming "3.5" is wrong; cite HearAdvisor's product page.

**ReSound Sensia** (US, 20 Aug 2026). No independent data. Automatic DNN is
**level 9 only**; Sensia 7 needs a manual program. Widest styles including
bimodal and CROS. Sources disagree on whether it replaces Vivia; ReSound has
not said Vivia is discontinued.

**Oticon Reveal** (announced 11 Aug 2026, orderable 24 Aug 2026). Speech AI +
Context AI running concurrently. miniRITE R only, IP68, four tiers, Tinnitus
SoundSupport on all of them, CROS transmitter alongside. **LE Audio, Auracast
and telecoil all working on day one** — genuinely ahead of Phonak and Widex,
and the live edge over Signia MaX.

**Signia MaX** (24 Aug 2026). Four DNNs under "Acoustic Intelligence". Pure
Charge&Go MaX, RIC only, eight colours. OneConnect = Bluetooth Classic + LE
Audio + telecoil + Auracast in one device. **Auracast hardware-only until a
firmware update Signia scheduled for March 2027.** No independent data, no
price, no published tiers, no IP rating, **no mention of tinnitus anywhere**.

**Signia IX** (3 Oct 2023). HearAdvisor **4.4/5** for the Pure Charge&Go BCT
IX; **Active Pro IX in the top 5% of everything tested**. Widest style range on
this list: Pure, BCT, Silk, Styletto, Motion M/P/SP, Active, Active Mini,
Insio IIC/CIC/ITC/ITE, with CROS across several. Tiers 7/5/3. Notch Therapy and
static/ocean-wave tinnitus signals standard. **Not discontinued** — Active Mini
IX shipped July 2026.

## Two studies worth citing on every cost page

- **Wu et al., *JAMA Otolaryngology*, 2025** — n=245, no significant outcome
  difference between devices costing roughly $4,400 and roughly $1,100.
- **Humes et al., *American Journal of Audiology*, 2017** — price had no
  measurable effect on outcome once the fitting was done well.

## On Costco

Do **not** claim they skip real-ear measurement — they list REM equipment as
standard. Also false in 2026: "last year's chip", and "nobody else can adjust
them" (unlocked since 2022). The defensible line is protocol transparency.
CHC's existing page is `/what-is-the-best-hearing-aid-sold-at-costco/`
("Warehouse-Club Hearing Aids"), which already absorbs about eight redirects —
**refresh it, do not build a competing page.**
